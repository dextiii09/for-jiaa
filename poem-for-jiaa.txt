Janamdin Mubarak, Jaan
— by Dhruv, for Jiaa

Tune pehli baar jo dekha mujhe,
aankhon mein kho gaya main kahin.
Us ek pal ki khaasiyat thi yeh —
duniya thamti lagi thi wahi.

Haath pakda tha jab tune mera,
ek ajeeb si kashish thi us touch mein.
Jaise sadiyon ki pehchaan thi koi,
apnapan tha chupta hua us pooch mein.

Teri awaaz thi meri subah,
teri yaad thi meri raat.
Tu hi mera chain tha, tu hi mera dard —
tu hi thi meri har baat.

Maine ki galtiyan, yeh sach hai, Laddu,
jhooth bola, dard diya.
Par yeh bhi sach hai — jo pyar tha mera,
woh asli tha, poora jiaya.

Aaj tera janamdin hai, jaan,
dua karta hun dil ki gehrai se.
Khush reh tu hamesha, muskurati reh —
sari khushiyan milein tujhe duniya bhar se.

Agar kabhi raah mile phir se,
aa jaana — main yahan hun.
Aur agar na mile — toh bas yeh jaanna:
teri khushi hi meri dua hai, Jiaa.

                              — Tera Dhruv 💕
